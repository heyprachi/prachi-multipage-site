Statistical Consulting

School of Nursing, Oakland University

  • I analyzed 3,000+ healthcare records to evaluate an emissions-reduction intervention achieving 45–75% reductions in fresh gas flow during anesthesia. This project helped Southwest Michigan hospitals meet national carbon compliance standards and reduce their overall carbon footprint.
  • I applied statistical expertise to Social Determinants of Health data to identify medical treatment gaps and social barriers facing vulnerable patient and veteran populations. This project informed equity-driven healthcare strategies across low-income Michigan communities.

Center for Statistical Training & Consulting (CSTAT), Michigan State University

  • I developed a custom R package, built an automated reporting system that cut reporting time by 80%, and delivered 24 technical reports on behavioural intervention trends for Michigan Department of Health and Human Services.
  • I also applied advanced statistical analyses to A/B test the effectiveness of reading interventions, providing data-driven recommendations that reached 50,000+ practitioners and researchers across Michigan's K-12 literacy programs.
