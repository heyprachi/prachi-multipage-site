Research

My academic projects primarily focus on judgement and decision-making using empirical data and experimental methods. I am also interested in assessing research integrity and reproducibility. I adopt a social-cognitive lens to explore questions like:

  • How can we quantify the accuracy of our judgements and decisions?
  • When are our decisions considered rational versus biased?
  • How and to what extent do our motivations and prejudices influence reasoning?
  • How do people use stereotypes when making predictions about others?
My current work largely evaluates multi-year, nationally-funded research projects across various domains.

Current Work

Transition to Organic Partnership Program (TOPP)

I support nationwide evaluation activities for USDA's organic agriculture program that has 165+ partner organizations, certified 260,000+ acres as organic, and served 3600+ mentoring relationships.

Innovation Corps (I-Corps™)

I am co-leading quantitative and qualitative analyses for National Science Foundation's I-Corps regional hub. Overall, this program has trained 2,500+ research teams, launched 1,400+ startups, and raised $3B+ in funding.

Science and Technologies for Phosphorous Sustainability (STEPS)

I am co-leading mixed methods research across 15+ institutions on National Science Foundation's convergence research program. This work helps validate research and learning outcomes, influencing $25M+ in future investments.

Precise Advanced Technologies and Health Systems for Under-resourced Populations (PATHS-UP)

My research assesses how effectively workforce development efforts, from K-12 to graduate students, prepare the next generation to bring afforadable healthcare solutions to underserved communities.
